How to Calculate Your Freelance Hourly Rate

Setting your freelance rate by simply dividing a full-time salary by 2,080 hours is one of the fastest ways to undercharge. Unlike salaried employees, freelancers pay self-employment taxes, buy their own equipment, and spend roughly 30% to 40% of their week on unpaid administrative work.

The Freelance Pricing Formula

  • Step 1 (Gross Target): Add your desired take-home pay and annual expenses, then adjust upward to cover income taxes.
  • Step 2 (Billable Capacity): Multiply realistic billable hours per week (usually 25 hours) by working weeks per year (usually 48 weeks).
  • Step 3 (Base Rate): Divide your Gross Target by your total annual billable hours to find your break-even minimum rate.

Frequently Asked Questions

Should I charge hourly or per project?

Use your hourly rate internally to estimate how much time a project requires, then quote the client a flat project fee. Flat project pricing rewards efficiency and gives clients predictable budgets.

What expenses should I include?

Include laptop replacement costs, internet bills, software subscriptions, accounting tools, coworking fees, health insurance, and marketing costs.
