Pricing Tips for Freelance Graphic Designers

Creative work involves heavy upfront thinking, mood-boarding, and client feedback rounds. If you charge strictly by the hour, getting faster at Adobe Illustrator or Figma actually lowers your income. Use this calculator to find your baseline hourly cost, then quote flat package prices with a strict limit of two revision rounds.
